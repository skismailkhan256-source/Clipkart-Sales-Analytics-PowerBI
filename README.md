# 📊 Clipkart Sales Analytics Dashboard — Power BI

An interactive **Sales Analytics Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze Clipkart's sales performance across revenue, orders, product categories, cities, quantity, and pricing.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of business performance and helps identify **top-performing products, high-revenue cities, category-wise revenue distribution, and the relationship between product price and revenue**.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to transform raw e-commerce sales data into an interactive and business-focused Power BI dashboard.

The dashboard is designed to answer key business questions such as:

* 💰 What is the total revenue generated?
* 🛒 How many orders were placed?
* 📦 What is the total quantity sold?
* 📈 What is the average order value?
* 🏆 Which products generate the highest revenue?
* 🌆 Which cities contribute the most revenue?
* 🏷️ Which product category performs best?
* 💵 How does product price relate to revenue?

## 📊 Dashboard Insights

### 1. Revenue by Category

The dashboard compares revenue generated across the three major categories:

* 👕 Clothing
* 📱 Electronics
* 🛒 Groceries

The category analysis provides a quick view of how revenue is distributed across the business.

### 2. Top Revenue Products

The dashboard highlights the highest-revenue products, including products such as:

* Bread
* Jacket
* Jeans
* Phone
* T-Shirt

This helps identify products that contribute significantly to overall revenue.

### 3. Top Performing Cities

The city-level analysis highlights the strongest revenue-generating locations.

The dashboard currently highlights:

* 🥇 Kolkata
* 🥈 Mumbai
* 🥉 Delhi

This provides useful insight into geographical sales performance.

### 4. Revenue Distribution

A donut chart shows the percentage contribution of each category to total revenue, making it easy to compare category performance visually.

### 5. Price vs Revenue

The **Price vs Revenue** visualization explores the relationship between product price and generated revenue.

This helps understand how revenue changes across different product price points.
